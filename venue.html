Getting there

Travel Information

🗺

By Air

Nearest airports: Trichy International (TRZ) ~55 km and Chennai (MAA) ~320 km. Taxis and buses connect to Thanjavur city.

🚆

By Rail

Thanjavur Junction is ~3 km from the venue. Auto rickshaws and taxis are readily available at the station.

🚗

By Road

Well-connected by state highways and frequent govt semi-sleeper & private bus services from all major cities.

🏟

Accommodation

For outstation guests: Sangam Hotel, Hotel Gnanam and Sree Venkateshwara are all within 3 km of the venue.

📍 Landmarks near the venue

Tamil Arasi Mahal is located on Pudukkottai Road, Parisutham Nagar — a short drive from the famous Brihadeeswarar Temple and the new bus stand. Watch for the large illuminated wedding banner at the entrance!
